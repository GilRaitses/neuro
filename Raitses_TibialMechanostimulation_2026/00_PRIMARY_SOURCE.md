# Primary Source

Cai et al.  
*Tibial bone compression promotes recovery after brain injury through osteocyte PIEZO1*  
Nature Neuroscience, 2026  
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02422-w

## Role in the white paper

This paper anchors the causal biological observation that motivates the project.

Round 1 should recover from the paper:

- loading parameters reported for each species and condition
- molecular and circulating readouts
- behavioral and injury-recovery readouts
- evidence supporting an osteocyte-dependent mechanism
- evidence supporting PIEZO1 involvement
- serum-transfer or other evidence for systemic mediation
- limits on what the study establishes
- questions left open about temporal stimulus structure

## Constraint

Do not infer a human loading force from the reported animal protocols.

The proposed research instrument should be described first as a programmable force-controlled benchtop platform. Human testing belongs to a later protocol after safety review and institutional oversight.
