## Section synthesis target

The literature should decide whether the working hypothesis survives:

A mechanical loading regime has biologically meaningful temporal structure, so protocols with similar cumulative exposure may produce different mechanotransductive states.

Possible formal object:

`R = {F_peak, preload, frequency, rise time, hold time, rest time, cycle count, bout structure, inter-bout interval, waveform}`

Do not assume every element is independently meaningful. Retain only parameters supported by the literature.
